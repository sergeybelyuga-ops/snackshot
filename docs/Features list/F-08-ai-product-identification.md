# F-08 | AI Product Identification

## Overview
Identify packaged products from an image and retrieve their nutrition details.

## User story
As a user, I want to scan a packaged product photo so I can log it accurately without manually searching for the product.

## Acceptance criteria
- The system detects a packaged product from the image.
- It extracts brand or product name and pack size.
- It matches the product to nutritional metadata.
- The user can confirm and save the result.

## MVP status
Necessary for MVP: No
