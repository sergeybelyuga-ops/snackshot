# Explore the Google Health app

Our mission is to help people live longer, healthier lives, and we're now taking our deep expertise in health and wellness to the next level by integrating the best of Google technology. The Google Health app brings together effortless tracking and adaptive coaching to help make the most out of every day.

With a Google Health Premium subscription, the Google Health Coach, built with Gemini, helps you build personalized plans and gives you proactive insights. It can also provide you daily feedback about your progress, and help connect the dots between your data and your daily life.

Tip: To get the latest features, keep your Google Health app updated to the most recent version. Google Health app and Google Health Coach are only available on versions 5.0 and above. Previous versions below 5.0 may experience issues.

## Get the app

Download and install the Google Health app from one of these locations:

- Android: [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fitbit.FitbitMobile)
- iPhone and iPad: [App Store](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/fitbit-activity-calorie-tracker/id462638897?mt=8&ign-mpt=uo%3D4)

To make sure you can use the Google Health app on your phone or tablet, go to [Google Health setup requirements](https://support.google.com/product-documentation/answer/14226283).

You can update the Google Health app the same way you update other apps on your phone. Your device may also automatically update your apps depending on your settings.

- [How to update apps on Android](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/113412)
- [How to manually update apps from the App Store](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102629)

If your apps update automatically, check "Updated recently" to know if the Google Health app was updated recently.

To check which version number of the Google Health app you have, tap the Today tab and then your profile picture and then Help. The app version is at the bottom.

## Get started

Use the Google Health app to log and track your fitness activities, your sleep, your health data and more. With a paired Fitbit device, Google Pixel Watch, or just your phone, see all your data in one place and track your progress and trends.

With Google Health Premium, unlock Google Health Coach, built with Gemini and made for you. It delivers proactive, personal, and adaptive guidance for the whole you.

At the top of the app, you can manage your connected devices and app settings.

1. Open the Google Health app.
2. Tap Connections and then your device.

At the top right of the app, tap your profile picture to adjust settings related to your account.

For more information, refer to:

- [What activities do my Fitbit device and Google Pixel Watch track](https://support.google.com/googlehealth/answer/14236510)
- [How to add, edit, or delete Fitbit data & activities](https://support.google.com/googlehealth/answer/14236402)
- [Track your steps with Google Health on your phone](https://support.google.com/googlehealth/answer/14236404)
- [How do I use GPS on my Fitbit device](https://support.google.com/googlehealth/answer/14225688)

## Navigate the app

For users with paired Fitbit devices or Google Pixel Watch, the Google Health app features 4 main tabs: Today, Fitness, Sleep, and Health.

For users without a Fitbit or Pixel Watch device paired, the Google Health app features 2 main tabs: Today and Health.

For more information, refer to [Feature map of the Google Health app](https://support.google.com/product-documentation/answer/17081467).

- Today tab
- Fitness tab
- Sleep tab
- Health tab
- Add friends in the Google Health app

## Google Health Coach built with Gemini (Google Health Premium Only)

Google Health Coach uses your health data, including information from your paired Fitbit devices, profile, and third-party apps, to provide personalized, proactive guidance and insights. You can ask your coach various wellness and support questions through the input bar.

Important: The Google Health Coach provides informational guidance, not medical advice. Consult a health care provider before making any health plan changes.

### Get personalized messages from Google Health Coach

You will receive messages that adapt to your daily activity.

- Morning: Check your sleep quality and physical readiness for the day.
- Post-workout: Review your performance breakdown and tips for improvement.
- Evening: Get a recap of how your body responded to your activities and progress toward your fitness plan.

What you can ask:

- Data Analysis: Get insights into your sleep, fitness, and health trends.
- Support and troubleshooting: Solve setup, device, or syncing issues.
- App navigation: Find specific metrics and features.

For more information, go to [Ask your Google Health Coach](https://support.google.com/googlehealth/answer/17053789).

To know more about personalized insights from Google Health Coach, go to [Learn about Google Health Coach](https://support.google.com/googlehealth/answer/16961408).
