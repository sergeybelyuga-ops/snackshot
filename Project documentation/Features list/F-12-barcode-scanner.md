# F-12 | Barcode Scanner

## Overview
Scan a product barcode to read nutrition values directly from a packaged item.

## User story
As a user, I want to scan a packaged good so I can log it accurately without manual searching.

## Acceptance criteria
- Barcode scanning is available from the app flow.
- The product is matched to known nutrition data.
- The result can be reviewed and saved to the nutrition log.

## MVP status
Necessary for MVP: No
