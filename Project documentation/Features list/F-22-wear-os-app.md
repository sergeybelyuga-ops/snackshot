# F-22 | Wear OS App

## Overview
Provide a smartwatch companion app for quick logging and review while on the go.

## User story
As a user, I want a quick way to log my intake from my smartwatch so tracking is less intrusive.

## Acceptance criteria
- The app provides key logging tasks on Wear OS devices.
- The smartwatch experience remains lightweight and simple.

## MVP status
Necessary for MVP: No
