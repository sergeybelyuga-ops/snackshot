# F-29 | Daily AI Health Recap

## Overview
Provide an automated daily summary of nutrition trends and habits.

## User story
As a user, I want a brief recap of my day so I can review my progress without manually inspecting data.

## Acceptance criteria
- The app can summarize the previous day's nutrition in a simple format.
- The recap is designed for readability and quick review.

## MVP status
Necessary for MVP: No
