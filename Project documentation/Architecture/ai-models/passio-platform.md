## Snap a Photo, Log Your Meals

Upload or snap a photo of your plate, and Photo Logging identifies everything it sees. It supports single food items like a fried egg and complex meals such as a strawberry salad. Users can tap to confirm or edit results before logging to their diary.

## Log Multiple Ingredients at Once

Scan multiple foods or ingredients in a single shot. No manual entry needed. This is intended for meal prep or batch-cooked meals and reduces logging fatigue.

## Scan Packaged Foods via Barcode

Scan a barcode to pull up exact brand and nutrition information. Over 3.5 million food products are supported via UPC.

## Read Nutrition Labels or Packaging

OCR pulls data directly from nutrition labels and food packaging. It automatically populates fields like calories, fats, sugars, and servings and recognizes common formats across snacks, drinks, and meal boxes.

## Log Foods By Speaking

### Natural Language Input

Say what you ate and the AI understands full meals and recipes.

### Multi-Food Parsing

Breaks down a sentence into structured, loggable items with quantity and calories when available.

### Keyboardless Logging

The voice flow is intended for driving, walking, or multitasking.

## Speak Naturally

Users can say full sentences like "I had scrambled egg whites, bacon, and whole-grain toast." The system handles the full sentence without requiring the user to pause, format, or break up the input. It works in any language supported by the mobile OS speech engine.

## Auto-Parse Multiple Foods

The voice transcript is automatically parsed into separate foods. Each item includes quantity, serving size, and calories when available. Users can select and unselect items before logging.
