Your data stays on your phone

Here is where your information is kept, what leaves your phone for food recognition, and what changes if you connect Google Health Connect.

smartphone

How Snackshot uses your information

Snackshot needs no registration and keeps no health data on its servers. Your meals, calories and macros are saved in the app's database on this phone and keep working offline.

  • cloud_off Not stored on Snackshot servers Customer profiles, nutrition logs, personal health data and photos
  • key AI access service To count your 90 free AI pictures and manage AI access, a small Snackshot service keeps an anonymous ID, a scrambled device identifier, the number of pictures used and your AI access status. Nothing that identifies you, and no photos or meals
photo_camera

AI food recognition

To recognize a meal, Snackshot sends only the picture you selected to LogMeal, the recognition service. It is not linked with your personal information.

  • check_circle Sent to LogMeal The one photo you took or picked from your gallery
  • block Never sent Your name, account, goals, meal history or other photos
  • history Kept on this phone The processed meal picture is kept with your meal for up to 365 days. The original capture is deleted after processing.
favorite

Google Health Connect

Health Connect is optional. Snackshot works fully without it, and your meals stay only on this phone.

  • account_circle Your Google account Connect with an existing Google account or create a new one
  • sync When connected New meals are saved to Health Connect too, which keeps your history and can share it with compatible apps you allow, for recommendations or other services
  • tune Your choice, any time Connect or disconnect later in Settings
